# Reviews

Session records. Each customer review or stakeholder deep-dive gets a file,
filled **live** and committed in the room.

The point is not minutes. The point is that the customer leaves with a
written, owned list of what was decided and what is still open — before
anyone has had a chance to remember it differently.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` — review notes for a specific session.

## Contents (recommended)

- Who was in the room.
- What was decided (each item traces to an ADR).
- What remains open (each item has an owner and a target date).
- What was demonstrated (with commit hashes).
- What was flagged (risks, disagreements, deferred choices).
