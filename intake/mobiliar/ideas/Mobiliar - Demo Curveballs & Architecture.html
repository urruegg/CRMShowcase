Demo Curveballs & Architecture Map

Die Mobiliar · Commercial CRM on Dynamics 365 / Dataverse — anticipated "challenge" use cases & the platform capabilities they probe

sports_baseballWhy this exists

During competitive demos, customers throw "curveball" scenarios — a single attribute change that should cascade into premium recalculation, coverage re-evaluation, or relationship handoff. They reveal whether the platform is genuinely model-driven or just a pretty UI.

Origin pattern (CSS / Krankenkasse): "Change of address to a different canton → premium recalculation." Under the KVG, basic-insurance premiums are rated by canton → premium region → age group → model, so a move legitimately forces a re-rate. Below are the equivalent — and arguably richer — curveballs for Mobiliar's P&C book.

Telephony architecture — Sales Hub Dialer (GA) vs Luware (Mobi24)

UC-AS1 happens at the Generalagentur, not at Mobi24 — so it is not a contact-center / ACD-queue journey. The two telephony stacks coexist on a single Teams Phone PSTN foundation but serve different populations and must be split by number/identity, never double-routing the same user's inbound through both.

warning

Coexistence watch-outs

Both stacks share one Teams Phone PSTN foundation
checklistPlan for

A Teams Phone user has one PSTN identity; the dialer needs dedicated seller numbers via resource accounts — allocate numbers so a GA seller's dialer line and any Luware queue membership don't collide on inbound. Pick one owner per number/flow (don't put the same agent behind a Luware ACD queue and the Sales Hub Dialer for the same inbound). In CH, PSTN connectivity is realistically Operator Connect or Direct Routing via Mobiliar's telco (Microsoft Calling Plans have limited Swiss availability) — the same connectivity Luware already uses, which simplifies the story.

campaignDemo framing

"GA Sales Support uses the D365 Sales-native dialer (screen-pop, auto-log, AI summary) — no contact-center seat — while Mobi24 keeps Luware for true ACD." Label the dialer preview and flag entitlement validation; don't imply GA-readiness or Contact Center licensing.
